From discovery to a useful shortlist

How to use this HipoBuy spreadsheet guide

01 / DISCOVER

Choose the exact item

Filter the catalog, then open CAFANS to locate the matching listing. Record its source URL, selected color, size and current price. Similar names can represent different options.

02 / VERIFY

Check evidence and fit

Compare the size chart with measurements from a garment you own. If ordering through HipoBuy, confirm the original marketplace link is supported and review current purchase terms before paying.

03 / REVIEW

Confirm the full cost

Keep item charges separate from domestic delivery, international shipping and other fees. Use your destination and actual parcel details when requesting a shipping quote.

A useful QC review starts with the selected variant

Check that the photographed item matches your ordered color, size and quantity. Look for visible damage, uneven seams, missing parts and differences from the listing. Ask for measurement photos when fit is uncertain. Catalog pictures and another buyer’s QC images do not prove the condition of your own item or establish authenticity.

Before arranging onward shipping, resolve important questions and check any applicable return deadline with the agent. Keep photos and order references together so that a later review has a clear record.

Clear answers

HipoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list used to organize finds, source links, prices and shopping notes for purchases someone may consider through HipoBuy. This page supplies a small CAFANS catalog selection and a comparison workflow; it is not an official HipoBuy spreadsheet.

Where do the products on this page come from?

The six displayed records come from the CAFANS homepage catalog. The names and indicative USD prices were transcribed for this page. Open CAFANS and confirm the current listing before making a decision.

Do these buttons buy an item directly?

No. “HipoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” both open https://cafans.com. Find the item there and review its detail page and available purchase options. This website does not take payments.

Are shipping and taxes included in the displayed prices?

The figures are indicative product prices, not delivered totals. Confirm delivery, agent charges, payment conversion and applicable destination charges separately. The planner adds only the amounts you enter.

Can every CAFANS product be ordered through HipoBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for this selection. Confirm the current marketplace link, product restrictions and available purchase options with HipoBuy before placing an order.

Are QC photos or stock verified here?

No. This page does not provide item-specific warehouse inspection evidence or live stock verification. Obtain current information for your exact selected option from the source and agent.

Can I search and filter without an account?

Yes. Search, category filters, price sorting and the budget planner run in your browser. The six catalog records remain readable when JavaScript is disabled.
